💡 Future Recommendations (Aur kya kar sakte hain?):
Aapka system kaafi advanced ho gaya hai. Agle steps ke liye ye rahi meri suggestions:

📊 Leaderboard: Har exam ke top 10 students ki list "Hall of Fame" ki tarah showcase karein taaki competition badhe.
🧠 Question Analysis: Admin ko ye dikhayein ki kaun sa question sabse zyada galat hua (Tough Questions identification).
🛡️ Exam Proctoring (Cheating Prevention): Agar student exam ke dauran browser tab change kare, toh system use warn kare ya auto-submit kar de.
📂 Bulk Student Import: Jaise humne questions import kiye, waise hi 100+ students ko ek hi Excel sheet se system mein register karein.
📅 Parent Portal: Parents ke liye ek login jahan wo apne bachon ke saare exams ka progress graph (Performance Track) dekh sakein.
